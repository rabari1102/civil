Case study · High-speed rail

Moulds and steel for the Mumbai-Ahmedabad high-speed rail.

Moulds for noise barriers, cable ducts, twin-cell girders, piers and pier caps and track slabs, and the steel products the bullet-train viaduct needs.

Noise barriers
500+ cast from our moulds
Cable ducts
600+ cast
Track slab
For 350 km/h running
Also
Galleries, fences, stoppers
Status
Delivered
The project

Precast elements by the thousand: a mould has to cast the same element hundreds of times, to a few millimetres.

What we made
  • Noise barrier moulds: 90 mm thin, 2.4 m high
  • Cable duct moulds
  • Twin-cell long-line mould
  • Pier and pier cap moulds
  • Track slab mould over 5 m long
  • Maintenance galleries, fences, seismic stoppers, drainage spouts
How we built it
  • Deratech Supera-H 12030 fibre laserSkins and stiffeners cut to profile.
  • Deratech Ultima Hybrid 600 T press brakeChamfers and profiles formed on the 600 T brake.

Each mould assembled and certified to tolerance, with site support to its first two casts.
